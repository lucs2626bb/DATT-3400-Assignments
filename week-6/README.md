# Week 6 moodboard:

Audio modifications for the dome projection

For the projection I have decided to use a float value in the switch TOP to transition between two renders at once “blending”. I was remined by the blending technique used in the music video Transmigration by Osamu Sato. He is a Japanese artist well known for using complicated designs with only basic 2D shapes. This video is what inspired me to make my dome presentaion like that.
